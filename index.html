Artefact Overview

Four-Wheel Mobile Motion System

The Four-Wheel Mobile Motion System is a mechatronic platform designed to achieve stable, controlled, and repeatable movement using four wheels. It integrates the chassis, drivetrain, motors, wheel kinematics, motion dynamics, and feedback control.

01 • STRUCTURE

Chassis and Stability

The chassis supports the wheels, motors, battery, electronics, and payload. Weight distribution, centre of gravity, wheelbase, track width, and rigidity influence stability during movement and turning.

02 • DRIVE

Four-Wheel Drivetrain

The four wheels provide support and traction. Motors generate torque, and the drivetrain transfers it to the wheels. Proper wheel arrangement ensures effective load distribution and ground contact.

03 • CONTROL

Drive Coordination

The controller coordinates wheel speed and direction to produce forward, reverse, turning, and stopping motions. In a differential-drive-type arrangement, different left- and right-side wheel speeds produce turning.

04 • MODELLING

Kinematics and Dynamics

Kinematics relates wheel motion to robot motion using wheel radius, wheelbase, track width, and velocity. Dynamics considers mass, payload, motor torque, traction, and resistance on robot motion and stability.

05 • FEEDBACK

Motor Control and Feedback

The motor driver controls motor power according to controller commands. Wheel encoders provide rotation and distance feedback, allowing comparison of desired and actual motion for improved accuracy and repeatability.

06 • VALIDATION

Performance Validation

The system is evaluated through experimental testing to verify speed, motion accuracy, turning behaviour, stability, distance accuracy, payload handling, and repeatability under defined operating conditions.
